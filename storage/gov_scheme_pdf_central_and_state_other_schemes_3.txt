FOREWORD
rd
As per Resolution passed in the 53  meeting of National Development Council (NDC) 
to launch a Food Security Mission comprising rice, wheat and pulses, the National Food 
Security Mission (NFSM) was launched during October, 2007.
The Mission met with an overwhelming success and achieved the targeted additional 
th
production of rice, wheat and pulses. The Mission continued during 12  Five Year Plan with 
th
new targets of additional production of foodgrains of 25 million tonnes by the end of 12  Plan. 
Coarse cereals and commercial crops were also included in the Mission. Considering the 
experiences and feedback received from the States, major changes were made in approach, 
norms of financial assistance and programme implementation strategy.
th
Based on past experiences and performance of 12  plan, it has been decided to 
th
continue the programme beyond 12  plan from 2017-18 to 2019-20, which is co-terminus with 
Fourteenth Finance Commission (FFC) period with new targets and modifications required. 
Based on the feedback from the states, with the approval of the General Council of the 
National Food Security Mission headed by the Union Minister of Agriculture & Farmers' 
Welfare, changes have been made to modify/include/clarify some of the guidelines, keeping 
in mind the greater interest of the farmers and demand from the States. From the year 2018- 
19, nutri-cereals, oilseed crops and Seed Village Programme have also been brought under 
the canopy of NFSM.
The focused approach of the Mission is to provide funds to beneficiaries through 
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) by the States. Provisioning of dedicated project management 
teams for catalyzing result oriented implementation will benefit millions of farmers across the 
country in the targeted States/Districts. 
The first and second editions of Operational Guidelines had been useful for the States 
in the preparation and implementation of the State Action Plans as per the Mission's 
objectives, programme interventions and norms of assistance. 
This revised booklet of operational guidelines of re-vamped NFSM containing all the 
modifications/clarifications is being brought out for the benefit of the field functionaries to 
ensure clarity in implementation of the programmes. 
I am sure DAC&FW and State Governments will disseminate these revised guidelines 
among all the stakeholders of the Mission including central & international agencies so as to 
enable them to contribute their utmost in this noble endeavor of ensuring food and nutritional 
security for the country.
