TITLE: TNAU Rice Irrigation and Water Management Guide (நெல் சாகுபடி நீர் மேலாண்மை)
CATEGORY: Rice_Paddy_Water_Management
=================================================================

1. Rice Irrigation Practices (நெல் பாசன முறைகள்):
- Initial establishment (நடவு பருவம்): Maintain shallow depth of 2 cm of water for the first 7 to 10 days to help seedling roots establish firmly.
- Tillering phase (தூர் கட்டும் பருவம்): Maintain water level between 2.5 cm and 5 cm. Avoid excessive flooding.
- Panicle Initiation & Flowering (பூக்கும் மற்றும் பால் பிடிக்கும் பருவம்): Critical moisture stage. Keep a steady 5 cm water depth to prevent unfilled grains.
- Pre-harvest drainage: Drain field completely 10 days before harvest to hasten maturity and ease mechanical harvesting.

2. Alternate Wetting and Drying - AWD / Pani Pipe Method (மாற்று உலர் மற்றும் நனைத்தல் முறை):
- Install a perforated plastic or bamboo tube (Pani pipe) into the field to observe water drop levels.
- Re-irrigate only when water level drops to 15 cm below soil surface.
- Saves up to 30% water without reducing yield and lowers field methane emissions.

3. System of Rice Intensification - SRI (செம்மை நெல் சாகுபடி):
- Practice intermittent irrigation (காய்ச்சலும் பாய்ச்சலுமாக பாசனம்) instead of continuous flooding.
- Irrigate when hairline cracks appear on the soil surface during vegetative stage.
